EXT. NEW CEMETERY - DAY

Two miles out. Two old people at a fresh grave, and nobody else.

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - BEDROOM - NIGHT

A week later. Darkness. Mr. White wakes alone. His wife is weeping at the window.

MR. WHITE
Come back. You will be cold.

MRS. WHITE
It is colder for my son.

Later, she sits bolt upright.

MRS. WHITE (CONT'D)
The paw! The monkey's paw!
(crossing to him)
The other two wishes. We've only had one. Go down and get it, and wish our boy alive again.

MR. WHITE
Good God, you are mad!

MRS. WHITE
Do you think I fear the child I have nursed?

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - PARLOUR - NIGHT

He gropes in the dark for the mantelpiece. His fingers find the paw.

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - BEDROOM - NIGHT

MRS. WHITE
Wish!

MR. WHITE
It is foolish and wicked.

MRS. WHITE
Wish!

MR. WHITE
I wish my son alive again.

Nothing. The candle gutters out. They lie listening to the clock.

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - STAIRS - NIGHT

Mr. White goes down for a candle. The match dies. A knock at the front door, so quiet it is almost not there.

He flees back upstairs. A third knock.

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - BEDROOM - NIGHT

MRS. WHITE
What's that?

MR. WHITE
A rat. It passed me on the stairs.

A loud knock.

MRS. WHITE
It's Herbert! It's Herbert!

He holds her arm. She tears free and runs down.

MR. WHITE
For God's sake don't let it in!

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - HALL - NIGHT

She drags back the chain and the bottom bolt. She can't reach the top one, so she drags a chair to the door.

MRS. WHITE
The bolt! Come down. I can't reach it.

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - BEDROOM - NIGHT

Mr. White on hands and knees, groping across the floor. The knocking is a fusillade now. His hand finds the paw.

He whispers something we don't hear.

The knocking stops.

EXT. LABURNAM VILLA - NIGHT

The door swings open on an empty road. A street lamp flickers. Mrs. White's long wail goes up into the wind.

FADE OUT.
