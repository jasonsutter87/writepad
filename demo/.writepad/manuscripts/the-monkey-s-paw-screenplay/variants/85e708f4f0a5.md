INT. LABURNAM VILLA - KITCHEN - MORNING

Winter sun. The paw lies tossed on the sideboard. Herbert grabs his coat.

HERBERT
Well, don't break into the money before I come back.

MRS. WHITE
(laughing)
Go on with you.

She watches him all the way down the road.

INT. MAW AND MEGGINS WORKS - DAY

Noise, steam, belts and wheels. Herbert works near a great flywheel. A belt slips. A shout we can't hear over the machines.

CUT TO:

EXT. LABURNAM VILLA - DAY

A well-dressed STRANGER in a new silk hat paces at the gate. Three times he stops and walks on. The fourth time he opens it.

INT. LABURNAM VILLA - PARLOUR - CONTINUOUS

Mrs. White hides her apron under a cushion and lets him in. He picks a thread from his trousers rather than look at her.

STRANGER
I was asked to call. I come from Maw and Meggins.

MRS. WHITE
Is anything the matter? Has anything happened to Herbert?

STRANGER
Badly hurt. But he is not in any pain.

MRS. WHITE
Oh, thank God! Thank God for that! Thank...

She understands. She takes her husband's hand.

STRANGER
He was caught in the machinery.

MR. WHITE
(dazed)
Caught in the machinery. Yes.

STRANGER
Maw and Meggins disclaim all responsibility. But in consideration of your son's services, they wish to present you with a certain sum as compensation.

MR. WHITE
How much?

STRANGER
Two hundred pounds.

Mrs. White shrieks. Mr. White smiles faintly, puts out his hands like a blind man, and falls.
