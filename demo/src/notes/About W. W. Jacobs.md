# About W. W. Jacobs

William Wymark Jacobs (1863–1943), English, born in Wapping. He was a clerk in the Post Office Savings Bank before writing full time. He is best known for comic stories about sailors, barges and dockside life; *Many Cargoes* (1896) made his name. That book is in this vault's Library.

*The Monkey's Paw* appeared in *Harper's Monthly* in 1902 and in his collection *The Lady of the Barge* the same year. It's his most famous story by far, and one of his few horror stories. It was adapted for the stage in 1903 and has been filmed many times.

Everything Jacobs published before 1929 is in the public domain in the US, and he died more than 70 years ago, so his work is public domain in the UK and EU too. That's why it can ship in a demo.
