# Why the knocks work

Craft notes on [[The knocking]], the most famous ending in short horror.

1. **We never see it.** Jacobs gives us sounds only: a knock "so quiet and stealthy as to be scarcely audible", then louder, then "a perfect fusillade". The reader builds the monster.
2. **The two-mile delay.** Herbert is buried in [[The new cemetery]], "some two miles distant". The gap between the second wish and the first knock is the walk. Mrs. White says it out loud: "I forgot it was two miles away."
3. **The ten days.** Mr. White's line "He has been dead ten days... I could only recognize him by his clothing" tells us what is walking up the road, without describing it.
4. **The bolt.** The top bolt Mrs. White can't reach buys exactly enough time for the third wish. A practical detail turns into the clock.
5. **The empty road.** The last image is nothing: "a quiet and deserted road." We never learn what the third wish was. We don't need to.
