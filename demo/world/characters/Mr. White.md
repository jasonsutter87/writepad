# Mr. White

Retired, gentle, a bad chess player who plays riskily and sulks about the road. He takes the paw out of the fire and makes all three wishes. Credulous, then ashamed of it, then terrified.

- Wants: nothing much. *"It seems to me I've got all I want."* That's the tragedy: the first wish is Herbert's idea.
- Last seen: running to the gate in [[The knocking]].
