# Adaptation notes

Working notes for **The Monkey's Paw (screenplay)**.

- **Three acts = three parts.** Jacobs already wrote it in three movements. Act breaks sit where his do.
- **Invented scenes:** the rainy exterior that opens the film, the cemetery, and INT. MAW AND MEGGINS WORKS. The story never shows the accident; a film might. **Book to script** flags the first two as having no clear source, and folds the factory into *Morning* as a short bridge. Is the factory worth it, or does it spoil the reveal for [[The man from Maw and Meggins]]?
- **Dialogue kept word for word:** "It moved", "Caught in the machinery", "Do you think I fear the child I have nursed?" Book to script finds these runs.
- **Cut:** most of Morris's war stories, the antimacassar chase, the tailor's bill.
- **The last wish stays silent:** "He whispers something we don't hear." Same as the story.
- **Voices for the table read:** Morris and Mr. White are British men, Mrs. White and the narrator British women, Herbert American (he's the modern one). Change them in **Cast**.
