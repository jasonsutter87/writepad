How the unlock works

  1. Buy Publish. You'll get a license key by email.
  2. In WRITEpad, open Settings → Publish and paste the key.
  3. Export → EPUB and Export → MP3 audiobook are now on, for every manuscript in every vault.

What's in the free version

Everything else: unlimited manuscripts, takes and cuts; onion skin; narration and dictation; Library, cadence and World; screenplays; git history; the MCP server; and export to PDF, Markdown and plain text. There's no trial and no nag screen.
