The software is provided **as is**, without warranty of any kind. Keep backups of
work that matters to you; WRITEpad's history feature and your own backups are the

## Your writing

What you write with WRITEpad is yours. We claim no rights to it, and we never receive
it. The same goes for every ebook and audiobook you export.

## The Publish unlock

Publish is a one-time purchase that turns on EPUB and MP3 audiobook export. Your license
key is for your own use on your own computers; please don't share or resell it.

Narration voices come from the Kokoro model. If you publish or sell an audiobook,
you're responsible for making sure your use of the voices and of your source text is
allowed.
