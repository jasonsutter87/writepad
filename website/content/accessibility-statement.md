We want WRITEpad and this website to be usable by everyone, and aim to meet the
[Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/)
at level AA.

On this site that means text that meets AA contrast in light and dark mode, a skip
link, navigation that works from the keyboard, visible focus, and images described
for screen readers.

In the app, narration can read any manuscript aloud, dictation lets you write by
voice, and themes, including custom colours with a contrast warning, let you set
the page the way you read best.

If something gets in your way, please [send us a message](/contact/) or
[open an issue](https://github.com/jasonsutter87/writepad/issues). We'll do our best to fix it.
