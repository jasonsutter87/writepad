The short version: your writing never comes to us. WRITEpad has no accounts, no
analytics and no telemetry, and this website sets no cookies.

## The app

WRITEpad works on a folder on your own computer. Your manuscripts, notes, versions,
history and Library measurements stay in that folder, and we have no copy of them.

The app connects to the internet only when you ask it to:

- **Setting up narration** downloads the Kokoro voice engine (from the Python Package
  Index) and its voice files (from GitHub).
- **Dictation** downloads a Whisper speech model the first time you use it. Speech is
  transcribed on your computer.
- **Adding a script to the Library from a link** fetches that page.
- **Polish with Claude** is off by default. If you turn it on, dictated text is sent to
  Anthropic with your own API key, under
  [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- **Agents** you connect through the MCP server act under their own provider's terms.

## This website

We don't run analytics or set cookies. To show the site, a few services see your IP
address the way any web server does:

- **Netlify** hosts the site.
- **Google Fonts** serves the typefaces.
- **GitHub** answers the download page's lookup of the latest release, and serves the files.

## The contact form

When you send a message, Netlify Forms stores your name, email address, topic and
message so we can reply. To stop the form
being flooded, we count messages per day from each address. We store a scrambled
(hashed) version of your IP address for that, never the address itself, and the
counts are kept only for the day.

## Buying Publish

When you buy the Publish unlock, the payment is handled by our checkout provider, who
collects your email address and payment details to process the order and send your
license key. We receive your email address and the order, and use them only to deliver
and support your purchase. We never see your card number and never sell your details.
