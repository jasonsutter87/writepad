**Onion skin** lays one version of a page over the one you're reading. Words you added are tinted. Words the other version had are drawn as faint ghosts, struck through, right where they were. You read the new version and see the old one underneath.

{{< shot "onion-skin" "The visitor compared with its tighter opening: added words tinted, cut words as ghosts" >}}

## Compare two versions

Open a page with more than one version. In the **Onion skin** panel on the right, pick a version under **Compare with**.

A line under the panel sums it up, for example *+124 words, −49 words vs tighter opening*.

## Fade the ghosts

Drag **Ghost** to fade the cut words in and out. All the way down shows just the version you're reading, with only the added words tinted.

## Hear the difference

**A/B listen** plays the compared version, then this one, so you can hear which reads better.

## Compare with an older save

With [History](/docs/history/) turned on, you can also compare a page with any saved version of it from the past, not only with its other takes.
