A manuscript is made of **pages**: chapters, scenes or sections, whatever suits the book. Open a manuscript from the **Manuscripts** tab and its outline appears in the sidebar, one row per page in book order.

{{< shot "outline" "The outline of The Monkey's Paw in the sidebar" >}}

Each row shows the page's title. If the page has more than one version, the row also shows how many, and which one is live, such as *2 · Jacobs 1902*.

## Open, add and rename

- **Click** a page to open it. With the keyboard, <kbd>Tab</kbd> to a page and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.
- **+ New chapter** adds an empty page after the one you're in, and opens it ready to type. In a screenplay it's a new scene.
- To **rename** a page, click its title at the top of the page and type.
- **‹** (*All manuscripts*) goes back to the list of manuscripts.

## Reorder

Drag a page by its row (or the **⋮⋮** grip) and drop it above or below another. A line shows where it will land.

## Front matter

Text before the first chapter heading, like a title page or an epigraph, is kept as **Front matter**. It's exported as it is, without a chapter heading.

## Leave a page out, or delete it

Hover over a page and click **−** to leave it out of the book for now, without deleting it. See [Cuts](/docs/cuts/).

To get rid of a page for good, open it and click **Delete page** next to its title, then click again to confirm. The page goes with every version it had. **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the window puts it back where it was. A manuscript always keeps at least one page.

## A word goal for the book

Set **Whole manuscript (words)** in your target (**Target…**, under the page title) and a progress bar appears under the manuscript's name in the sidebar, for example *32,410 of 80,000 words*. It counts the live version of each page in the book, and skips pages that are left out.
