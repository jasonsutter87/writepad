WRITEpad keeps everything in a **vault**: an ordinary folder on your computer. Notes are plain markdown files you could open in any editor, including Obsidian. Manuscripts, their versions and your settings for them live in a `.writepad` folder inside it. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Shortcuts on these pages say <kbd>⌘</kbd>. On Windows, use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>.

## The first time

The first time you open WRITEpad, it offers three ways in:

- **Try the demo**: a short story, *The Monkey's Paw*, set up to show every feature. WRITEpad puts a copy in `Documents/WRITEpad Demo` and opens its **Start here** note. Change anything you like; it's your copy.
- **Start a new book**: makes a `WRITEpad` folder in your Documents and opens a blank manuscript.
- **Open a folder…**: any folder of markdown, including an existing Obsidian vault or drafts you already have.

To switch to another vault later, open **Settings** (top right) and click **Change…** next to **Vault**. The vault's name shows at the top left of the window.

{{< tip >}}
**Try the demo first.** Every feature in these pages can be tried on it. (It's also on the [download page](/download/) as a zip.)
{{< /tip >}}

## The four tabs

The sidebar has a search box and four tabs:

- **Manuscripts**: your books and scripts. Click one to open its outline.
- **Notes**: every markdown note in the vault, with **New note**.
- **World**: characters, places, things and the timeline. See [Your story world](/docs/your-story-world/).
- **Library**: books you measure your writing against. See [Library and targets](/docs/library-and-targets/).

## Start a manuscript or a screenplay

On the **Manuscripts** tab, click **New…**. Give it a **Title** and pick:

- **Manuscript**: chapters of prose.
- **Screenplay**: scenes, cues and dialogue. <kbd>Tab</kbd> changes a line's type.

Click **Create** and it opens, ready to type.

{{< shot "new-manuscript" "The New dialog with a title and the Manuscript and Screenplay choices" >}}

## Bring in drafts you already have

On the **Manuscripts** tab, click **Import…**, then **Choose files…**. You can pick every draft of the same work at once (markdown, EPUB or PDF):

- An EPUB becomes one page per chapter.
- A PDF is split on its headings.
- In markdown, each `## heading` starts a page. Sections with the same heading in different files line up as **versions** of that page, oldest first, and the newest becomes the live one.

Give it a **Title** and click **Import**.
