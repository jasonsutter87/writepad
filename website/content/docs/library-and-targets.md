The **Library** measures published books so you can see how your draft compares: how long their chapters run, how long their sentences are, how much is dialogue, how chapters end. Blend the ones you admire into a **target**, and WRITEpad measures every page you write against it.

Only the numbers are kept, never the books' text.

## Add books

On the **Library** tab, click **Add books…** and pick EPUB files. Each one is measured and added to the list.

Each book has a **weight**: how much it counts in your target. Set it to `0` to keep a book in the library without it counting. **×** removes a book (click twice).

## Compare

Click **Compare** to see your manuscript against the target, measure by measure: chapter lengths, sentence lengths, dialogue, how chapters end, repeated openers and more. A line at the top sums it up, like *on target in 2 of 11 measures. Biggest gaps: …*. It updates as you write.

## While you write

The line under each page's versions measures that page against the target:

> **577** words (target chapters 503–743) · **16** median sentence (target 11) · **32%** dialogue (target 24%)

With a Library target, **marks** underlines the places where the page drifts from it. Hover over one to see why; click the button to **hide** or **show** them.

## Your own numbers

Not everything has to come from other books. Click **Target…** (or **Set a target…**) under any page's title to set numbers for this manuscript:

- **Chapter length**, from and to (words)
- **Median sentence** (words)
- **Dialogue** (%)
- **Whole manuscript** (words), which also shows a progress bar in the sidebar

Any you leave blank fall back to your Library blend, if you have one. **Clear** removes them all.

## Screenplays

The **Scripts** section of the Library measures screenplays the same way. See [Screenplays](/docs/screenplays/).
