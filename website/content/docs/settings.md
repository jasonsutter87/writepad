Click **Settings** at the top right. Changes take effect when you click **Save**.

{{< shot "settings" "The Settings dialog" >}}

## Vault

The folder WRITEpad works in. **Change…** opens a different one. See [Your first vault](/docs/your-first-vault/).

## Narration

- **Set up narration** installs the voices (about 350 MB, needs Python 3.10 or newer).
- **Voice for *italic* lines**: the second voice, for thoughts and other italics.
- **Pause after paragraphs (seconds)**.

If you already have Kokoro installed, point **Python with kokoro-onnx** and **Kokoro model folder** at it instead. See [Listen and table reads](/docs/listen/).

## Appearance

**Theme**: **Match system**, **Light**, **Dark** or **Custom**.

**Custom** lets you make your own: start from a preset or from **Light** or **Dark** (*Based on*), then pick colors for the **Sidebar**, **Background**, **Page**, **Text** and **Accent**. WRITEpad warns you if text would be hard to read against its background.

## Dictation

The speech model, language and optional polish. See [Dictation](/docs/dictation/).

## Updates

WRITEpad checks for new versions a few times a day. When one is ready, a card in the corner offers **What's new** and **Update now**. Every update is signed, and your work is saved before it installs (with a [history](/docs/history/) snapshot, if history is on).

**Check for updates** looks right away.
