WRITEpad writes screenplays as well as prose. A screenplay is a manuscript whose pages are scenes.

## Start one

On the **Manuscripts** tab, click **New…**, pick **Screenplay**, give it a title and click **Create**.

## Write

Type, and press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to change a line's type: scene heading, action, character, dialogue and so on. The formatting bar reminds you: *Screenplay · Tab changes a line's type · ▶ Listen for a table read*.

Screenplays get everything prose does: [versions](/docs/versions/), [onion skin](/docs/onion-skin/), [cuts](/docs/cuts/) and [export](/docs/export/).

## Table read

**▶ Listen** reads a screenplay with a different voice for each character, and a narrator for headings and action. Change who reads what in the **Cast** panel on the right. See [Listen and table reads](/docs/listen/).

## Learn from produced scripts

In the **Library** tab, under **Scripts**, click **+** (*Add a screenplay*). Paste a link to a script page (IMSDb works) or **Choose a file…** (.txt, .fountain or .pdf), then click **Add**. WRITEpad measures it and keeps the numbers.

**Open** copies a script into your vault as a manuscript, so you can read it or hear it as a table read.
