**🎙 Dictate** turns speech into text at the cursor. Transcription runs on your own computer with Whisper.

## Dictate

Open a page or a note and put the cursor where the words should go. Then either:

- **Hold Right ⌥** (the Option key on the right; **Right Alt** on Windows) and talk. Let go, and the text lands at the cursor.
- **Tap** it, or click **🎙 Dictate**, to go hands-free: it keeps listening until you tap again.

The button shows **● Listening** while it records and **… Writing** while it transcribes. Pressing another key while holding Right ⌥ cancels.

## Settings

In **Settings**, under **Dictation**:

- **Whisper model**: the speech model to use, such as `small`. Bigger models are more accurate and slower.
- **Language**: like `en` or `es`, or `auto` to detect it.
- **Polish with Claude: drop ums and fix punctuation, keep every word**. This is off by default. When it's on, the transcript is sent to Anthropic's Claude to tidy it, which needs an Anthropic API key and an internet connection. Without it, the raw transcript is inserted.
