**Export** (top bar) writes out the manuscript you have open, as it stands: its pages in outline order, the ★ live version of each, and none of the pages [left out of the cut](/docs/cuts/). With a note open instead, it exports the note.

## Formats

| Format | Good for |
| --- | --- |
| **Word** (.docx) | Word, Pages and Google Docs; sending to an editor or agent |
| **PDF** | Print-ready, 5.5 × 8.5 inch pages |
| **EPUB** | E-readers |
| **OpenDocument** (.odt) | LibreOffice |
| **Rich Text** (.rtf) | Opens almost anywhere |
| **Web page** (.html) | Reading in a browser or posting online |
| **Markdown** | Other writing tools |
| **Plain text** | Anything |
| **MP3 audiobook** | One narrated file per chapter; a screenplay is read by its cast |

Word, OpenDocument and Rich Text files get a title page with your byline, and each chapter starts on a new page. [Page properties](/docs/page-properties/) are never exported.

## Export

1. Click **Export**. The dialog says exactly what will go out, such as *The current cut of "The Monkey's Paw": 6 pages … 2 left out.*
2. Pick a format.
3. Fill in **Author** for the byline.
4. Click **Export…** and choose where to save.

An MP3 audiobook is narrated on your computer and can take a while; progress shows in the dialog.
