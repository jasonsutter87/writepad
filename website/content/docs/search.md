The search box at the top of the sidebar looks through the whole vault: every note, every version of every page (not just the live ones), and page [properties](/docs/page-properties/).

{{< shot "search" "Search results for a phrase, with matches highlighted" >}}

## Search

Click the box, or press <kbd>⌘⇧F</kbd>, and type. Results appear as you type.

- **Several words** find places that have all of them.
- Each result shows the page or note, where it is, and the matching text with your words highlighted.
- Results with your words in the title, or the exact phrase, come first.

Click a result to open it, with the first match selected. <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the top result. <kbd>Esc</kbd> clears the search.

Because search looks in every version, it's also how you find the draft where you wrote that line you cut.

## Search by reference

Type a reference like `Matthew 1:1-2`, `John 3:16` or `Luke 15` and WRITEpad finds the pages whose properties cite an overlapping passage. Book names can be shortened, like `matt 5:3`, and any kind of dash works in a range.
