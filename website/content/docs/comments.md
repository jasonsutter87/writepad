**Comments** are notes about the text that aren't part of it: "is this the house's name or the street's?", "cut this?", "check the date". They sit in the **Comments** panel on the right, and the words they're about are highlighted in the page. They're never exported and never change your text.

## Add one

Select some words, then click **+ Comment on selection** (or press <kbd>⌘⌥M</kbd>). Type your note and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>. <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> starts a new line in the note; <kbd>Esc</kbd> cancels.

## Read and answer

- **Click highlighted words** in the page to find their comment in the panel.
- **Click the quoted words** at the top of a comment to jump to them in the page.
- **Click a comment's text** to edit it.
- **Resolve** when you've dealt with it: the highlight goes, and the comment moves to **Resolved**, where **Reopen** brings it back.
- **×** deletes it (click twice).

## When the text changes

A comment follows its words as you write around them. If you rewrite the words themselves, WRITEpad looks for them again; if they're gone, the comment stays in the panel with a note that the words it was on have changed.

Each version of a page has its own comments, so a note on the draft doesn't show up on the rewrite.

## Comments from AI agents

AI agents working through WRITEpad's agent tools can leave comments too, marked with a robot and the agent's name. It's a good way to get an editor's notes without letting anything rewrite your words. You decide what to do with each one.
