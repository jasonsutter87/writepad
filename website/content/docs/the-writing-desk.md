With a manuscript open, WRITEpad has four parts.

{{< shot "desk" "WRITEpad with The Monkey's Paw open: sidebar, page and panels" >}}

## The top bar

- **The vault's name** (top left): click it to open a different vault. Beside it, **words today** counts what you've written today across every page and note (cutting words counts against it).
- **🎙 Dictate**: talk and the words land at the cursor. See [Dictation](/docs/dictation/).
- **▶ Listen**: reads aloud from the cursor, or just the selection (<kbd>⌘↵</kbd>). <kbd>Esc</kbd> stops. Next to it are the narrator **voice** and the **speed**. See [Listen and table reads](/docs/listen/).
- **Export**: Word, PDF, EPUB and more. See [Export](/docs/export/).
- **Settings**: your vault, narration, theme, dictation and updates. See [Settings](/docs/settings/).
- **?**: opens the help page for what you're looking at, in your browser. The **Help** menu (<kbd>⌘?</kbd>) does the same, and has the blog and **Report a bug…**.

Each panel on the right has a small **?** beside its heading that opens the help page for that panel.

## The sidebar

The **search box** (<kbd>⌘⇧F</kbd>) searches every note and every version of every page. See [Search](/docs/search/).

Below it are the **Manuscripts**, **Notes**, **World** and **Library** tabs. With a manuscript open, **Manuscripts** shows its outline: one row per page, in book order. See [Pages and chapters](/docs/pages-and-chapters/).

## The page

At the top: the page's **title**, its **versions** (see [Versions of a page](/docs/versions/)), a line of numbers about the page, and its **properties** (see [Page properties](/docs/page-properties/)).

Then the formatting bar:

| Button | What it does | Shortcut |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **B** | Bold | <kbd>⌘B</kbd> |
| *I* | Italic | <kbd>⌘I</kbd> |
| **Left** | Align left | <kbd>⌘⇧L</kbd> |
| **Center** | Center the line | <kbd>⌘⇧E</kbd> |
| **Right** | Align right | <kbd>⌘⇧R</kbd> |

Next to them, **Focus** hides everything but the page, with no sidebar, panels or onion skin (<kbd>⌘⇧D</kbd>, or **View → Focus mode**). <kbd>Esc</kbd> brings it all back. When you select text, the bar shows how many words are selected.

On the right of the bar, pick how the page looks:

- **Page**: a sheet of paper, like a word processor.
- **Draft**: just the words.
- **Code**: monospace with line numbers, for seeing the markdown underneath.

Everything you type is saved as you go. Misspelled words are underlined by your computer's spell checker; right-click one for suggestions.

**Find and replace** in the page you're on: <kbd>⌘F</kbd> opens it at the top of the page. Type what to find, <kbd>Enter</kbd> or **Next** steps through the matches, and **Replace** or **Replace all** swaps in the text from the second box. To look through the whole vault instead, use [Search](/docs/search/).

## The panel on the right

- **Onion skin**: compare this version with another. See [Onion skin](/docs/onion-skin/).
- **Comments**: notes on the text. See [Comments](/docs/comments/).
- **In this scene**: the characters, places and things the page mentions, and the timeline events it's linked to. See [Your story world](/docs/your-story-world/).
- **Cuts**: saved versions of the whole book. See [Cuts](/docs/cuts/).
- **History**: snapshots of the vault. See [History](/docs/history/).
- **Pronunciations**: how the narrator says names. See [Listen and table reads](/docs/listen/).
