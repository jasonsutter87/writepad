Every page can have several **versions** (takes). Try a tighter opening, a different point of view or a new ending without losing what you had. The versions sit side by side above the text.

{{< shot "versions" "Two versions of The visitor above the text, with + Fork and Delete" >}}

## Fork a version

Click **+ Fork** (*Copy this version to try something different*). WRITEpad copies the version you're looking at and opens the copy, compared against the original (see [Onion skin](/docs/onion-skin/)). Rewrite the copy; the original stays exactly as it was.

## Switch, rename and choose

- **Click** a version to open it.
- **Double-click** a version to rename it. Name it for the idea, like "tighter opening" or "Herbert's POV".
- The version marked **★** is **live**: it's the one that goes in the book, the export and the word count. Open another version and click **★ Make live** to use it instead.

Versions written by an AI agent through WRITEpad's agent tools are marked with a robot and the agent's name. An agent can only add its own versions. Making one live is always your call.

## Delete a version

Click **Delete** on the version you're looking at, then click again to confirm. **Undo** in the message at the bottom of the window puts it back where it was. A page always keeps at least one version.

## The page's numbers

Under the versions, a line measures the page you're on:

- **Words**, with the chapter length you're aiming for.
- **Median sentence** length, in words.
- **Dialogue**: the share of words inside quotation marks.

The targets come from your [Library](/docs/library-and-targets/), or from numbers you set yourself with **Target…** (**Set a target…** until you have one). With a Library target, a **marks** button underlines the places where the page drifts from it; hover one to see why. Click it to **hide** or **show** them.
